# Claims and evidence

| Claim | Status | Evidence and dependencies |
|---|---|---|
| The free diagonal cyclic quotient is closed, smooth and symplectic in dimension eight. | Proved, internally reviewed | Product symplectic form, finite free deck action, Kotschick's Theorem 2. |
| The descended circle action is effective and locally free with stabilizers 1 and Cₘ. | Proved, internally reviewed | Freeness on the first factor and axial sphere rotation. |
| The action is non-Hamiltonian. | Proved, internally reviewed | Extremum of a Hamiltonian on a closed manifold would be a fixed point. |
| Nonpolar geometric orbits are contractible. Polar geometric orbit classes have exact order m. | Proved, internally reviewed | Primitive lifts, cyclic deck transformations and contractibility upstairs. |
| Examples persist in dimensions 8+2k. | Proved, internally reviewed | Product with trivially acted-on spheres and retraction. |
| Deck endpoints for 2≤m≤64 agree with the model. | Computational | [Exact replay](verification/src/replay.py). |
| Six-dimensional mixed example. | Open in this work | No six-dimensional example follows from the construction. |
