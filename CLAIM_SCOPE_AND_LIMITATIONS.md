# Scope of the result

For each integer $m ≥ 2$ there exists an effective locally free symplectic circle action on a closed connected symplectic manifold of dimension $8+2k$ for each $k ≥ 0$. The principal orbit inclusion is null-homotopic, and each exceptional orbit inclusion has exact order $m$ in the fundamental group. All full acting-circle orbit maps are null-homotopic. The theorem addresses Henigman and Karshon's dimension-unrestricted Question 8.2. Its proof leaves the dimension-six refinement open.

Pelayo's finite diagonal quotient already exhibits a free and an exceptional stratum. The argument here uses Kotschick's contractible upstairs orbit and computes the fundamental-group classes of the geometric orbits after quotienting. The literature review gives a bounded assessment of overlap. It certifies neither historical priority nor external acceptance.
